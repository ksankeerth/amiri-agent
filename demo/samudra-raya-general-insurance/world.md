# Samudra Raya General Insurance: the world it is set in

Samudra Raya General Insurance is invented. The world around it is real, as of **16 October 2026**. The monitors in
`12 New monitor/` and the scenarios in `13 Write your own scenario/` are written for this situation.

## Indonesia
- MSCI has opened a consultation on moving Indonesia from emerging-market to standalone status. Feedback is due by
  30 April 2027, with a decision by the end of June 2027. The share index fell sharply.
- The rupiah is near 18,000 to the US dollar, and Bank Indonesia is intervening.

## The Gulf and Hormuz
- Ships around the Strait of Hormuz have been attacked since March 2026, and the US blockades Iranian ports. On
  11 October there were 2 transits, against about 85 a day before the crisis.
- War-risk premiums for Gulf voyages rose from 1 to 3% of hull value to 7.5 to 10% by late July (Marsh).
- Brent crude is around $104 to $106 a barrel.

## US rates
- US consumer prices rose 3.7% in the year to September, more than forecast. The Federal Reserve meets on 27 and
  28 October. Asian currencies are sliding.

## Weather
- The rainy season is starting, and the northwest Pacific typhoon season is very active.
