# Serendib Commercial Bank: the world it is set in

Serendib Commercial Bank is invented. The world around it is real, as of **16 October 2026**. The monitors in
`12 New monitor/` and the scenarios in `13 Write your own scenario/` are written for this situation.

## Sri Lanka
- Inflation was 8% in August (World Bank). The policy rate has been 8.75% since May.
- The rupee was about 331 to the US dollar on 15 October.
- Remittances were up about 20% on the year to August. Fuel imports were up 68% in July.

## The Gulf and Hormuz
- Ships around the Strait of Hormuz have been attacked since March 2026, and the US blockades Iranian ports. On
  11 October there were 2 transits, against about 85 a day before the crisis. A container ship and a bulk carrier
  were reported sunk.
- Brent crude is around $104 to $106 a barrel. IEA members are releasing oil from their reserves.

## US rates
- US consumer prices rose 3.7% in the year to September, more than forecast. The Federal Reserve meets on 27 and
  28 October; markets are split between a rate rise then and one in December. Asian currencies are sliding.

## Weather
- October to December is the northeast monsoon and the Bay of Bengal cyclone season.
